# Talking Ai
1. when i speak, , the program needs to transcribe what i say to text and feed it to a local model
2. the local model thinks and spits out an answer
3. the answer is then transcribed to audio and played

### additional features (for later implementation):
* it would be best if i can interrupt it anytime.
*

***
## tools to use for implementing that
* recording audio: [sounddevice](https://python-sounddevice.readthedocs.io/en/0.5.3/usage.html) and [soundfile](https://pypi.org/project/soundfile/)
* speech to text: [openai whisper](https://python-sounddevice.readthedocs.io/en/0.5.3/usage.html)
* the ai itself: [ollama](https://pypi.org/project/ollama/) im going to use the model [mistral 7b](https://ollama.com/library/mistral:7b)
* text to speech: [pyttsx3](https://pypi.org/project/pyttsx3/)
***

it is bestpractice to make the entire thing modular, or else, as the project grows, it is going to be a little overwhelming to manage and remember everything
